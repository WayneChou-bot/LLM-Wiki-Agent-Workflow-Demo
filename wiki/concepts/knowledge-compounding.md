# Knowledge Compounding

## Summary

The core claim of the LLM Wiki pattern: knowledge should be compiled once and then kept current, so every later ingest, question, and answer builds on everything that came before. Plain RAG rediscovers from scratch on every query; a maintained wiki accumulates.

## Cross-Page Insights

- From `LLM Wiki` (raw): "the wiki is a persistent, compounding artifact" — the cross-references already exist and the contradictions are already flagged before any question is asked.
- From `Karpathy LLM Wiki Summary`: a good answer should not disappear into chat history; archiving syntheses back into the wiki is what makes exploration compound like ingested sources do.
- From `Deliberate Practice Notes`: the same loop applied to skills — practice generates the experience, the knowledge layer keeps it durable. Compounding needs a capture step, not just repetition.
- From the Personal Knowledge lens: prefer repeated small ingests over large, unreviewed dumps — cadence beats volume.
- From the Project Management lens: decisions and risks tracked in the wiki survive beyond any single demo or meeting.
